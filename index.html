Percy S. Zhai

 

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Econometrics and Statistics at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. I received an M.S. in Statistics from the University of Chicago and a B.S. in Physics from the Special Class for the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China.

Research Interests

  • Time series: covariance and precision matrix inference under long-range dependence, conformal prediction

  • Bayesian inference: horseshoe priors, predictive inference

  • Graphical models: high-dimensional Gaussian and functional graphical models

  • Simulation-based inference: conditional flow matching, generative posterior samplers
